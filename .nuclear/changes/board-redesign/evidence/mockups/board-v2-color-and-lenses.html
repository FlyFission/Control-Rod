Color, filters and lenses

Everything on this board is live: click the kind filter, the toggles, "Color by" and "Group by". Code and documents each carry one gentle hue on the card edge, label and a faint tint. Amber stays reserved for what needs you and violet for independent checks, so meaning never collides.

Why a thin edge, not a colored card

Full color cards turn a board into a quilt. A 3 px edge plus a few percent tint is readable at a glance across a room, and leaves strong color free for the two things that must shout: needs you and independent check.

One board, four questions

Color by kind asks "what is the mix?". By state, "where is it stuck?". By agent, "is Codex or Claude doing better here?". By person, "who is overloaded?". Filters and color are separate, so you can filter to documents and still color by person.

What a saved view would add

Any mix of filters, color and grouping can be saved as a named view, like "My checks" or "Code in flight", and pinned for the team. That is how Linear and Notion keep one board useful for many people.
