The board is a plausible organizing surface, but this proposal is not ready to become a specification. Its strongest screens promise controls the current system does not demonstrate, including live steering, an enforced dollar cap, and signing past a failed check. The ledger also promotes product observations and design judgments into “Strong” evidence without establishing demand among the intended users. Keep the owner’s board-centered direction and interactive lenses, but first establish the product’s actual trust boundaries, review semantics, and a testable small-team workflow.

1. **Critical: A failing check can be signed through and the card becomes Done.** The document mockup says a current-source check is failing, offers “Sign with passkey,” and claims that a reason makes signing acceptable. Its click handler immediately sets the signed state, which says the card moved to Done. That contradicts V3’s proposed enforced blockers and gives a reviewer a misleading route around an engineering basis problem. The prototype does not even collect the promised reason. **Evidence:** [ledger V3](../00-decision-ledger.md:58); [document sign-off and failed check](../mockups/board-v3-card-and-review.html:247); [sign handler](../mockups/board-v3-card-and-review.html:299). **Fix:** Define which checks block acceptance, distinguish a signed *failed or inconclusive verification* from approval, and refuse acceptance server-side while a mandatory check fails. If exceptions are allowed, define their authority, scope, evidence, and separate signed disposition before drawing the UI.

2. **Critical: “Anyone but the requester” is weaker than the existing independence rule and does not establish a distinct person.** Reassigning the card must not erase authorship. The current verification code identifies originators from capture and package-authoring events, checks qualification, and rejects an originator; the mockup describes only the requester exclusion. A second account or credential controlled by the same human could still defeat an account-ID comparison unless enrollment and organizational controls establish person identity. This is a threat to assess, not a demonstrated bypass of the current implementation. A solo user also cannot honestly complete an independent check alone. **Evidence:** [person-verification.ts](/home/flyfission/worktrees/control-rod-signin/services/api/src/person-verification.ts:47), [admissibility checks](/home/flyfission/worktrees/control-rod-signin/services/api/src/person-verification.ts:92); [mockup wording](../mockups/board-v3-card-and-review.html:249). **Fix:** Preserve the owner’s independent-check requirement while binding eligibility to the immutable originator history and an authorized, qualified member. Specify enrollment, duplicate identities, delegated administration, credential recovery, and the organizational trust assumption. Keep independent work pending when no eligible second person is available.

3. **Critical: Review strictness can become a downgrade path.** The ledger makes strictness a card field but does not say who may change it, when, or what happens to existing approvals. Changing ownership, copying a card, or switching from independent to light review after work starts could remove the intended gate unless the server binds the requirement to the work and its history. The owner’s choice of light review as the default does not authorize lowering an applicable rigor or overlay floor. **Evidence:** [ledger C2 and V4](../00-decision-ledger.md:34); [ADR 0015](/home/flyfission/worktrees/control-rod-signin/docs/adr/0015-modes-rigor-overlays.md:7). **Fix:** Specify authorized changes, signed rationale where required, inherited policy floors, revisioning, and invalidation of affected approvals. Carry originator and strictness history through retries, copies, reassignments, and exported evidence.

4. **Critical: The signature and proof language overstates what a reviewer has signed or proven.** The mockup says the passkey is recorded “with the document digest” and implies a signed card plus exportable evidence. The implemented person-verification digest binds an act, package ID and revision, decision-subject digest, author run, criteria, and outcome. It is not simply a signature on a displayed PDF or DOCX byte digest. A signature also establishes a cryptographic binding to a credential and subject, not that every engineering value is correct or every isolation rule held. **Evidence:** [mockup sign-off](../mockups/board-v3-card-and-review.html:247); [verification subject](/home/flyfission/worktrees/control-rod-signin/services/api/src/person-verification.ts:114); [chain seal subject](/home/flyfission/worktrees/control-rod-signin/services/api/src/seals.ts:40). **Fix:** Show the exact decision, outcome, criteria, package revision, subject digest, and linked artifact digests before signing. Specify how source bytes, output bytes, checks, and the displayed rendition are bound, and recheck freshness atomically at signing and acceptance. Label unverified evidence and the limits of offline verification.

5. **Critical: The dollar cap is presented as a guarantee the current accounting cannot enforce.** The running card says “$0.84 of $5 cap” and “Stops on its own at the cap.” The API explicitly says current subscription runtimes do not report cost or have a pinned price schedule; USD and tokens cannot be hard capped, while only reserved runtime minutes can be. Per-card scope is also absent from the listed organization, project, and agent budget scopes. **Evidence:** [mockup budget](../mockups/board-v3-card-and-review.html:187); [budgets.ts](/home/flyfission/worktrees/control-rod-signin/services/api/src/budgets.ts:9), [scope and cap validation](/home/flyfission/worktrees/control-rod-signin/services/api/src/budgets.ts:84). **Fix:** Display available measured usage and uncertainty, and offer only enforceable reserved-minute limits initially. Treat a per-card monetary hard cap as a separate backend and provider capability project requiring pricing provenance, reservations, concurrent-run accounting, overshoot bounds, and reconciliation tests.

6. **Major: Queue, “Steer now,” and quota resume conflict with the accepted run model.** The mockup promises to interrupt the current step; R1 promises pause and resume on provider quota. ADR 0019 describes one-shot runs against a frozen package revision, and ADR 0020 describes a single prompt and event stream to exit. Research establishes demand for interactive workflows, not that this runtime supports their semantics. Resuming a queued, not-yet-started run is materially different from resuming a stopped provider session. **Evidence:** [ledger R1 and R4](../00-decision-ledger.md:43); [mockup steering](../mockups/board-v3-card-and-review.html:175); [ADR 0019](/home/flyfission/worktrees/control-rod-signin/docs/adr/0019-own-workbench-clean-room.md:15); [ADR 0020](/home/flyfission/worktrees/control-rod-signin/docs/adr/0020-the-mvp-daemon-runs-agents-in-headless-print-mode-acp-is-deferred.md:6). **Fix:** For the existing model, offer stop with confirmed outcome and a separately authorized follow-up run against a newly frozen subject. Park undispatched work on quota. If live interaction is desired, first revise the run protocol and threat model, including input provenance, authorization, isolation, interruption, retries, and what the signed subject means.

7. **Major: The screen assumes approvals and attestation exist at every point in the agent’s execution.** “Exact command or file” approval, “sandbox attested for every run,” and “rules held” are stronger than the cited accepted MVP ADR, which says print-mode runs have no ACP permission binding and actions are observed from the stream and outputs. Later sandbox work may alter individual capabilities, so the ADR alone is not proof of every current runtime’s behavior. The redesign must derive its claims from the actual run’s recorded mode and evidence. **Evidence:** [ledger R3 and S1](../00-decision-ledger.md:11); [mockup checks](../mockups/board-v3-card-and-review.html:274); [ADR 0020 conditions](/home/flyfission/worktrees/control-rod-signin/docs/adr/0020-the-mvp-daemon-runs-agents-in-headless-print-mode-acp-is-deferred.md:12); [workbench design disclosure flags](/home/flyfission/worktrees/control-rod-signin/docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-29-workbench-ontology-design.md:405). **Fix:** Create an evidence-to-display matrix by runtime and run: requested versus achieved isolation, attestation result, permission mediation, missing evidence, and staleness. Display “not established” where the record cannot support a checkmark.

8. **Major: S1 is a strategic hypothesis presented as a settled market fact.** The source itself calls its interpretation judgment. [Bloop’s announcement](https://www.vibekanban.com/blog/shutdown) says the company closed but Vibe Kanban continues as a community-maintained local project. [Conductor’s announcement](https://www.conductor.build/announcement) describes joining Meta and a wind-down, not evidence that customers rejected parallel work or bought governance. The research also describes active alternatives and platform consolidation. Neither closures nor enterprise vendor positioning demonstrate that small teams will pay for proof, that “every funded survivor” sells governance, or that proof and useful parallelism are substitutes. **Evidence:** [ledger S1](../00-decision-ledger.md:11); [research interpretation and product statuses](../03-agent-products.md:15). **Fix:** Regrade the strategy as an owner hypothesis. Interview and observe the actual owner and engineers on a code task and an engineering document; measure time to a trustworthy review, friction, and willingness to maintain evidence. Position proof as a differentiator only to the extent those tests support it, while retaining useful concurrency and review ergonomics.

9. **Major: Several evidence grades are stronger or more specific than their sources.** Examples:

   - **B1:** “Board as home” is Strong, but [research 02](../02-developer-planning.md:25) also identifies a separate triage inbox for externally created work. Folding all attention into a board filter is a product decision, not proven convergence.
   - **B3:** The ledger attributes four categories to Linear, while [research 02](../02-developer-planning.md:65) lists Triage and Backlog as well. A simplified mapping can be chosen, but should not be called Linear’s full model.
   - **B7 and B8:** Aging and rework are extrapolated from a small number of flow products; [research 06](../06-oss-board-demand.md:79) grades aging weak. “Rework rate is the agent quality signal” and “Review will be the bottleneck” omit task difficulty, reviewer availability, and revision causes.
   - **R4 and V2:** Demand for live chat does not validate the exact Queue and Steer protocol, and general passage commenting does not demonstrate reliable DOCX and PDF annotation. [Research 05](../05-oss-agent-demand.md:108) supports the broader needs, not those implementations.
   - **S2:** Complaints support offline local work; “no account for local use, ever” is an absolute product policy that does not settle local identity, shared-team authentication, or signed-review enrollment.

   **Fix:** Regrade each compound row by component, distinguish vendor features, issue demand, observed behavior, and Control Rod judgment, and record negative evidence. Put actual owner usability findings beside market evidence before labeling an exact default Strong.

10. **Major: The ledger suppresses material open decisions and misses stronger demand signals.** It says no business calls remain open, yet review downgrade authority, solo independent review, sign-off exceptions, account identity, integration priority, and enforcement scope remain undecided. The OSS survey says integrations and authentication dominate reactions, and identifies card relations and multiple assignees among leading board-specific requests. A “blocked” label without a dependency relationship does not tell an engineer what must finish first. **Evidence:** [ledger owner decisions](../00-decision-ledger.md:90); [research 06 rankings](../06-oss-board-demand.md:164). **Fix:** Maintain an explicit open-decisions register. Test import, repository and PR linkage, dependency relationships, and handoff between requester and qualified reviewer before committing to calendar, health metrics, or decorative aging.

11. **Major: The board’s state model conflates operational facts with draggable workflow labels.** A user moving a card to Running cannot itself prove a dispatch succeeded; moving to Done cannot establish acceptance. A blocked run, a pending approval, a lost daemon, and a failed check have different recovery paths. A concurrency limit with “allow with a reason” could also be mistaken for permission to exceed an actual runtime or safety limit. **Evidence:** [ledger B2, B5, B6](../00-decision-ledger.md:19); [current board](/home/flyfission/worktrees/control-rod-signin/apps/web/src/pages/Board.tsx:6); [run waiting causes](/home/flyfission/worktrees/control-rod-signin/spec/work.tsp:163). **Fix:** Define cards as projections of authoritative package, run, and decision states. Treat drag or Move to as a request that can be refused with an actionable reason; update the card only on confirmed events. Separate a soft visual WIP threshold from hard dispatch and resource constraints.

12. **Major: The document review is a styled HTML example, not a demonstrated engineering-document workflow.** The sample has highlighted paragraphs and a placeholder for four more pages. It does not show scanned PDFs, tables, drawings, equations, tracked changes, embedded objects, page rotation, mixed page sizes, inaccessible text layers, or a native DOCX file faithfully rendered. Text-quote and paragraph-index anchors can become ambiguous after revisions or duplicate text; silent reattachment can associate a finding with the wrong engineering statement. **Evidence:** [mockup document](../mockups/board-v3-card-and-review.html:254); [research 07 DOCX limitations](../07-building-blocks.md:153); [existing PDF review design](/home/flyfission/worktrees/control-rod-signin/docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-23-domain-deliverables-design.md:16). **Fix:** Review the exact retained file with its digest, page and coordinate convention, revision-bound annotations, and explicit stale or orphaned anchors. Provide the original download and an approved external-review path when fidelity or accessibility cannot be established. Validate against representative engineering documents, including scanned and equation-heavy examples, before promising in-app sign-off.

13. **Major: Accessibility and dense-card behavior remain design claims, not demonstrated properties.** In all three visual directions, small muted IDs, counts, and cost labels are difficult to read; the warm-paper and light variants also use fine borders and small colored text. The screenshots alone do not establish a contrast failure. The v2 lens assigns color to kind, state, agent, or person while amber and violet communicate other meanings, increasing the chance that the same edge or hue is interpreted inconsistently. The card packs status, owner, agent, review class, cost, time, and actions into a small area. In v3, clickable document highlights are spans handled by a document click listener, with no demonstrated keyboard interaction. Reduced motion is considered for the pulse, but does not establish the whole workflow’s accessibility. **Evidence:** [v2 lens controls](../mockups/board-v2-color-and-lenses.html:125); [v3 interactive spans and handler](../mockups/board-v3-card-and-review.html:253); [v3 reduced-motion rule](../mockups/board-v3-card-and-review.html:314). **Fix:** Keep the lenses, but provide persistent text or icon labels for every meaning, a clear active legend, and a compact configurable card face. Measure contrast in every theme and state, test keyboard-only operation and screen readers, preserve focus after updates, test zoom and narrow layouts, and honor reduced motion for all transitions.

14. **Major: Dependency choices are conditional experiments, not “reuse, do not build” decisions.** The listed permissive direct licenses appear compatible with the repository rule, subject to checking exact versions, notices, transitive dependencies, and distribution obligations. They do not supply the evidence model or safe review behavior:

   - **dnd-kit:** Classic packages are older, and the research records multi-list, variable-height, announcement, and Firefox issues. Built-in keyboard support is not proof that this board works with virtualization and multiple columns. [Evidence](../07-building-blocks.md:42).
   - **Base UI:** A headless primitive still requires correct composition, focus, labels, and real assistive-technology testing; issue counts and downloads do not prove accessibility. [Evidence](../07-building-blocks.md:65).
   - **@pierre/diffs:** Annotation hooks do not solve stable revision-aware comment identity, large-diff fidelity, keyboard review, or trust in displayed changes. [Evidence](../07-building-blocks.md:110).
   - **react-pdf and pdf.js:** The proposed anchor layer is owned product code, including coordinate transforms, text-layer failures, and orphan handling. Calling it small is unvalidated. [Evidence](../07-building-blocks.md:129).
   - **ProseMirror:** It is an editing framework, not a complete review, revision, or persistence system. The research itself recommends skipping it for review-only Markdown. [Evidence](../07-building-blocks.md:144).
   - **docx-preview and Mammoth:** Known chart, page-break, equation, and numbering limitations undermine an unqualified faithful-rendering claim. A fallback that drops layout can also change review meaning. [Evidence](../07-building-blocks.md:153).

   **Fix:** Keep the four proposed spikes and add a representative DOCX fidelity spike. Freeze versions only after license and transitive-tree review, offline and security checks, accessibility tests, and documented fallback criteria. Do not let a rendering library define the authoritative artifact.

15. **Major: The feasibility boundary is understated.** A visual board, filters, grouping, a non-drag move menu, and read-only display of existing records are comparatively contained web work, though still subject to data and accessibility testing. Durable card creation, lifecycle transitions, comments and anchors, live run controls, per-card budgets, independent-review policy, signed acceptance, and exportable evidence require varying degrees of API, persistence, contract, runtime, and potentially policy or kernel work. The existing board is a projection of packages, not an implementation of the proposed writable card lifecycle. **Evidence:** [current Board.tsx](/home/flyfission/worktrees/control-rod-signin/apps/web/src/pages/Board.tsx:6); [repository rules](/home/flyfission/worktrees/control-rod-signin/AGENTS.md:5); [ADR 0019](/home/flyfission/worktrees/control-rod-signin/docs/adr/0019-own-workbench-clean-room.md:15). **Fix:** Produce a feature-by-feature impact map with existing capability, proposed contract, migration, tests, and owner. Rule 2 requires a human signature for changes to named trust surfaces, including kernel decision functions and major spec versions; do not assume every ordinary API or UI change independently requires that signature. Amend conflicting ADRs and regenerate contract-derived code when applicable.

16. **Major: The product lacks an operator-grade recovery and evidence path.** A strong competitor offers repository linkage, review context, conflict handling, workspace lifecycle, and reliable recovery alongside agent execution. A regulated engineering team will additionally expect controlled source revisions, qualification, traceable numerical inputs and outputs, review disposition, tamper-evident records, and a way to inspect the actual deliverable and evidence after export. A green check saying “every value traced” is not a substitute for inspecting the mappings or handling untraceable values. The research already identifies lost work, stale state, and merge conflicts as concrete failure classes. **Evidence:** [research 05 failure modes](../05-oss-agent-demand.md:161); [domain deliverables design](/home/flyfission/worktrees/control-rod-signin/docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-23-domain-deliverables-design.md:21); [mockup evidence claims](../mockups/board-v3-card-and-review.html:263). **Fix:** Define recovery from restart, lost run, stale event stream, failed export, base drift, and orphaned workspace. Demonstrate a complete evidence bundle with source and artifact digests, tool-run inputs and outputs, review criteria, signer eligibility, unresolved findings, and verification instructions. Make missing or partial evidence visible as such.

**Three changes required before writing a spec**

1. Resolve and document the authoritative review and acceptance rules: identity, originators, qualifications, strictness changes, failed checks, exact signed subjects, and solo-user behavior.
2. Replace the mockup’s unsupported execution and cost guarantees with an evidenced capability matrix for each runtime, then decide explicitly whether interactive runs and per-card monetary caps justify changes to the accepted architecture.
3. Regrade the ledger by individual claim and validate one complete code task and one representative engineering-document task with the intended users, including accessibility, failure recovery, and evidence inspection.
