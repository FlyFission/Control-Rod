Before a spec is written, I would insist on three changes:

1. **Draw the run boundary in the ledger.** Print-mode, one-shot, frozen inputs stay the default. Steer, in-run questions, pause and resume, and a mid-turn dollar kill are removed or explicitly deferred behind an owner-signed ADR 0020 change. The spec is not allowed to say "interrupts the current step" or "stops on its own at the cap."
2. **Write the signature and the separation rule to match the kernel, then extend the kernel only with a signature.** The button binds the `subjectOf` digest, in words, and stays off when basis or required checks fail. Requester, grade, and strictness are kernel inputs. The requester cannot relax them. Solo use has a defined blocked outcome. No client-side "viewing as" switch counts as the control.
3. **Re-grade the ledger from files 05 and 06, and put the missing rows on it.** S1 becomes a bet, not Strong. Calendar outranks list. Creation includes repo, base branch, and tracker import. Add multi-repo, worktree lifetime, grade, person verification, and hardware-key eligibility. Columns follow kernel statuses. Drag does not decide.
