1. **High: [review panel design:116](/home/flyfission/worktrees/control-rod-panel/docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-06-review-panel-design.md:116)** permits an existing pre-check with different inputs to count as a panel seat. Showing the mismatch does not establish that the run reviewed the panel’s frozen snapshot, used its finding schema, or passed Stage 1. It can therefore contribute to quorum and vendor counts for a review it did not perform. Link such a run as separate pre-check evidence, or count it as a panel seat only after verifying that its inputs, output contract, and validation match the request. Test the mismatch case to ensure it cannot satisfy panel quorum.

2. **High: [review panel design:116](/home/flyfission/worktrees/control-rod-panel/docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-06-review-panel-design.md:116)** replaces the pinned pre-check’s job inputs with the post-policy snapshot and brief, but the existing pre-check job also supplies the package, preparation inputs, acceptance criteria, schemas, and author deliverables. The completion path records the verdict against the full author outputs. If the content policy excludes a deliverable or necessary criterion, the checker could issue a verdict without seeing what that verdict covers. Specify the complete inputs required for the existing pre-check contract, apply the content policy and confirmation to them, and refuse or limit pre-check completion when required inputs are withheld. Test a withheld deliverable and a prepared package.

3. **Medium: [review panel design:68](/home/flyfission/worktrees/control-rod-panel/docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-06-review-panel-design.md:68)** binds confirmation to the snapshots, diff, policy result, lane, and recipients, but not to the brief subsequently built with request intent and precedents at [line 89](/home/flyfission/worktrees/control-rod-panel/docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-06-review-panel-design.md:89). A changed brief can therefore be sent under an earlier confirmation even though it is provider-bound content. Build and scan the final brief before confirmation, include its digest in the confirmation, and reject dispatch if it changes. Test changes to intent and precedents after confirmation.
