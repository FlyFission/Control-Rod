The simple login experience is sound as a product goal, but the proposed security boundary is not yet established. It can be sound with fixes if the authenticated CLI is treated as a trusted component and every agent-controlled capability is explicitly constrained, including tools, extensions, environments, sockets, configuration, and privileged consumers of workspace files. The current spike supports selected command restrictions on one WSL2 setup; it does not justify the claim that all agent actions occupy a separate domain. Keep the decision conditional until full tool-path qualification and fail-closed policy enforcement exist.
