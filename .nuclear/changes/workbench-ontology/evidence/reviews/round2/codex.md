Revision 1 is not ready for approval as a complete execution plan. Several accepted findings are only partially resolved, and the new grade, clearance and theme provisions introduce enforcement gaps. The theme restrictions express the intended boundary, but neither the rendering contract nor Phase 4 sequencing reliably enforces it. Phase 0 and the spikes can receive separate, bounded authorization after correcting the approval dependencies.
