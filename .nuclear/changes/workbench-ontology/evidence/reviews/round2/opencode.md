## Office view and themes (spec 13, 18; plan Phase 4)

The theme contract is sound. The allowlist (scene art, furniture, figure styling within the five marks, ambient palette, ambient motion, flavour text with a plain name always available) and the denylist (states, shapes, marks, lens scales, room meaning and position, SCRAM, needs-me count, case view, decision bar, digests, refusals, signature surfaces) together with N-13 make "themes never change meaning or signing surfaces" a structural property rather than a hope: themes are scene-only packages, palette tokens are restricted to scene colours, and geometry must conform to the canonical layout graph. It is testable as planned: contract validation (4.6) is a mechanical allowlist check, and the harness (4.7) renders scenes for contrast, shape legibility at minimum zoom, frame budget and a reduced-motion variant, with a recorded manual pass. Two soft spots remain: figure styling "within the five marks" leans entirely on the legibility test to catch recognizability regressions, and room-name flavour text needs the plain-name guarantee asserted in the harness. Sequencing is right in the large: engine and harness land with the floor renderer (4.3, 4.6, 4.7) before any catalogue theme (4.8), so gates exist before the first real theme, and the catalogue is honestly marked pending owner decision D-7 with open question Q-B, since no catalogue exists in any read source. The defects are the contradictory D-7 gating (N-03, N-09) and the unverified donor claims adjacent to the office work (N-04, N-10).
