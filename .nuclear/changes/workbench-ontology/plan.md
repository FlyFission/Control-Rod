# Plan: workbench and ontology

Review the [specification](../../../docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-29-workbench-ontology-design.md) and the [buildout plan](../../../docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-29-workbench-buildout.md). Collect independent reviews from four outside CLIs into `evidence/reviews/`, adjudicate every finding (accept, reject with reason, or defer), revise, and present to the owner with decisions D-1 to D-7. Two approvals: Authorization A covers Phase 0 and spikes S-0 to S-5 (S-0 first; each spike run on the owner's go ahead); Authorization B covers Phases 1 to 9 and requires decisions D-1 to D-7 (the D-7 catalogue list gates only catalogue production). Protected edits require the owner's signature before the edit.

## Exit criteria

The specification, plan and donor register are reviewed, Authorization A is recorded for Phase 0 and spikes, and Authorization B for Phases 1 to 9 once D-1 to D-6 and the D-7 theme contract are recorded (the D-7 catalogue list gates only plan task 4.9), and no planned capability is presented as implemented.
