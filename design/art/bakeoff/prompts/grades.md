## Each grader's criteria

### Codex (gpt-6.2-astra)

1. The finished art registers to the blockout: same 30° projection, scale, margins, corners and occluding edges, with no camera drift.
2. plausible realism a nuclear-plant veteran would accept
3. Line weight, flat fills and selective section hatching make the image read as an engineering drawing at thumbnail size.
4. The core is the site’s only glow; room screens are legible by shape and value, not by bloom or alarm colours.
5. All 13 pads and the front half of each room floor stay quiet enough for composited crews, labels and counters.
6. Each prompt stands alone and specifies the attachment, aspect ratio, final frame and treatment of pixels outside the asset.

### Claude (claude-fable-5-2)

1. **Geometry lock**: the prompt treats the blockout as an exact tracing layer (same canvas, framing, 30° isometric, footprints, wall heights, ring, spurs) and explicitly forbids zoom, crop, re-centring and added margins.
2. **Plausible realism a nuclear-plant veteran would accept**: a PWR power reactor with the right equipment in the right building (vessel, head, steam generators, pumps, pressuriser and polar crane inside containment; boards, mimic and supervisor's desk in the control room), and nothing misplaced.
3. **Schematic GA-sheet language**: hairlines, flat tonal fills, section hatching on cut faces, centre lines, dimension ticks and leader callouts, stated as a drawing convention rather than a mood.
4. **Palette and light discipline**: named hex roles, cyan on navy, no warm hue, the core as the only glow, and screens always dimmer than the core.
5. **Overlay readiness**: calm, low-contrast mid-tones, empty pads, a clear front half of each room floor, no people, and no readable text, numbers or logos.
6. **Model fit**: one self-contained narrative prompt per asset, written for Gemini image-to-image: it says what the attached image is, what to keep, what to replace, gives the reason for each rule, and specifies a keyable background for tiles.

### Antigravity (gemini-4-neon-high)

1. **Exact blockout geometry lock**: Preserve the attached reference's 30° isometric projection, canvas bounds, wall heights, front occlusion lips, ring road, pipe spurs, and pad footprints with zero zoom, crop, or perspective drift.
2. **Plausible realism a nuclear-plant veteran would accept**: Depict authentic commercial PWR containment systems, main control room layouts, and engineering work-bay hardware in their proper plant areas without research-reactor clichés or security-specific details.
3. **Cohesive schematic GA-sheet aesthetic**: Enforce crisp hairline cyan-and-white technical drafting, flat tonal fills, 45° architectural section hatching on cut edges, and restrained non-readable drafting marks—never painterly game art or sci-fi renders.
4. **Strict palette and illumination discipline**: Restrict all colours to cyan-on-navy with zero warm hues (no red, orange, amber, yellow, or magenta), reserving true glow solely for the reactor core and faint muted screen-cyan for displays.
5. **UI-ready negative space and compositing cleanliness**: Leave all 13 site pads blank and empty, keep the front half of every room floor clear and low-contrast for crew sprites and overlays, render all text unreadable, and keep tile exteriors cleanly keyable/transparent with zero spill.
6. **Model-aware image-to-image execution**: Bind directly to the attached blockout with positive visual phrasing, explicit removal of reference text labels, and opaque-JPEG flat-background handling for Gemini Nano Banana that also transfers cleanly to Codex `gpt-image`.

## Each grader's verdict line

- **Codex (gpt-6.2-astra):** **Best: P3.** Borrow P1’s four-loop specification—one pump per loop and one shared hot-leg pressurizer—but show it in section, not staged on the deck.
- **Claude (claude-fable-5-2):** **P1 is best, narrowly (48 vs 47 for P3); the single most valuable fix is P3's framing lock: pre-pad the blockout to the output ratio with its exact row counts, state the fit rule and named anchor shapes in the prompt, and take the tile silhouette from the blockout alpha instead of a magenta key.**
- **Antigravity (gemini-4-neon-high):** **P3** is best, and the single most valuable fix from the others is grafting **P2's PWR containment and MCR realism details** (properly scaled RPV inside a concrete biological shield with bolted head and CRDM housings, steam generators in concrete cubicles, RCPs, a single pressurizer, plus overhead MCR annunciators and a centered SRO sightline) onto P3's geometry-locked prompts.
