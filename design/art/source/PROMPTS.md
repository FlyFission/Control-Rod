## core-tripped is a composite

Both generations of core-tripped came back zoomed about 2.2% about the top-left corner. Rooms drifted by up to about 40 px against base-dark, so neither raw output could be crossfaded. The committed core-tripped.webp was made by a script. It fits a uniform scale and translation from the generated image to base-dark, using edge maps outside the containment: scale 1.0220, translation −1.0 px, −1.0 px on the 2400 canvas. That fit lifts edge correlation outside the containment from 0.243 to 0.925. The script then inverse-warps the generated image and pastes only its containment (the anchors.json core bbox plus 40 px, with a feathered elliptical edge) onto base-dark. Everything outside the containment is therefore base-dark itself. The second attempt is the one used.

## Crew: crew-working, crew-waiting, crew-stuck, crew-needs-you

All four are cut from one character sheet (four poses of the same operator, side by side, same scale, transparent background), so the design and scale are identical across poses. The sheet was split at fully transparent column gaps, and each figure cropped to its alpha bounds. ONE common scale factor was applied to all four, so relative head heights follow the pose and the standing figure is tallest. Each figure was placed on a 512×512 transparent canvas with its lowest opaque pixel on y=496 and centred horizontally. Output of `normalise_crew.py`:

```text
common scale factor: 0.7028 (sheet 2172x724; tallest figure 683px; widest 344px)
crew-working: sheet crop (199, 68, 496, 687) 297x619 -> 209x435 at scale 0.7028; opaque rows y=63..496 (head top 63, baseline 496)
crew-waiting: sheet crop (680, 63, 1024, 684) 344x621 -> 242x436 at scale 0.7028; opaque rows y=62..496 (head top 62, baseline 496)
crew-stuck: sheet crop (1195, 184, 1493, 686) 298x502 -> 209x353 at scale 0.7028; opaque rows y=145..496 (head top 145, baseline 496)
```
