## core-tripped is a composite

Both generations of core-tripped came back zoomed about 2.2% about the top-left corner. Rooms drifted by up to about 40 px against base-dark, so neither raw output could be crossfaded. The committed core-tripped.webp was made by a script. It fits a uniform scale and translation from the generated image to base-dark, using edge maps outside the containment: scale 1.0220, translation −1.0 px, −1.0 px on the 2400 canvas. That fit lifts edge correlation outside the containment from 0.243 to 0.925. The script then inverse-warps the generated image and pastes only its containment (the anchors.json core bbox plus 40 px, with a feathered elliptical edge) onto base-dark. Everything outside the containment is therefore base-dark itself. The second attempt is the one used.

# Site plate and room tiles

- Site plate: painted image-to-image from `design/art/blockout/site-2400x1500.png` and `site-390.png`, with the native B-healthy key frame as style reference. Chain: site-lit and site-tripped are edits of site-dark. Each painted plate was fitted to its reference by `fit_affine.py`, an axis-aligned affine that maximises edge correlation. site-dark was fitted to the site blockout. site-lit and site-tripped were fitted to the fitted site-dark. site-tripped then had only its containment (anchors core bbox + 40 px, feathered ellipse) composited onto site-dark. The phone crop was fitted to `site-390.png`. Encoded with `cwebp -q 90 -exact`.
- Room tiles: painted from each `design/art/blockout/tiles/<tile>.png` blockout with transparent_background=true. Lit first; dark is an edit of lit. `fit_tiles.py` fits each painted tile onto its blockout canvas (axis-aligned affine maximising alpha-mask IoU) and warps it to the exact canvas, without clipping. The front layer is the fitted lit tile with its alpha multiplied by the `-front.png` blockout alpha. Encoded with `cwebp -q 90 -alpha_q 100 -exact`.
