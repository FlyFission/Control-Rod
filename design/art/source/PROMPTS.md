## core-tripped is a composite

Both generations of core-tripped came back zoomed about 2.2% about the top-left corner. Rooms drifted by up to about 40 px against base-dark, so neither raw output could be crossfaded. The committed core-tripped.webp was made by a script. It fits a uniform scale and translation from the generated image to base-dark, using edge maps outside the containment: scale 1.0220, translation −1.0 px, −1.0 px on the 2400 canvas. That fit lifts edge correlation outside the containment from 0.243 to 0.925. The script then inverse-warps the generated image and pastes only its containment (the anchors.json core bbox plus 40 px, with a feathered elliptical edge) onto base-dark. Everything outside the containment is therefore base-dark itself. The second attempt is the one used.

## Crew (radworkers): crew-working, crew-waiting, crew-stuck, crew-needs-you, crew-walking

All five are cut from one character sheet (five poses of the same radworker, side by side, same scale, transparent background), so the design and scale are identical across poses. The sheet was split at fully transparent column gaps, and each figure cropped to its alpha bounds. ONE common scale factor was applied to all five, so relative head heights follow the pose and the standing figure is tallest. Each figure was placed on a 512×512 transparent canvas with its lowest opaque pixel on y=496 and centred horizontally. Output of `normalise_crew.py`:

```text
common scale factor: 0.7306 (sheet 2172x724; tallest figure 657px; widest 334px)
crew-working: sheet crop (73, 108, 367, 673) 294x565 -> 215x413 at scale 0.7306; opaque rows y=84..496 (head top 84, baseline 496)
```
