# Room tiles: adding rooms and archetypes

The Plant is one painted **site plate** (ground, containment and core, ring road, pipelines, an empty flat pad per room) plus one painted **room tile** per archetype x size, composited by the app at each pad. A new room costs a pad and an existing tile, not new art. Everything below is generated by `python3 tools/render_blockout.py`; no number is typed by hand.

## Placement rule

`tiles/tiles.json` gives each tile its canvas size, `scale` (tile px per plate px, 2) and `pad_anchor` (tile-local px of the floor's back corner). `anchors.json` gives each room its `archetype`, `size`, `tile` and `pad` (plate px of the same corner).

- Tile top-left on the 2400x1500 plate = `pad - pad_anchor / scale`; draw it at `1/scale` of its pixel size.
- Seat feet = tile top-left + `seat / scale`. Draw `<tile>-front.png` above figures for occlusion, `<tile>.png` below.

## Add a room

1. Add its id and `(archetype, size)` to `ROOM_ARCH` in `tools/render_blockout.py` and its place on a ring (or use `ring_slots`). Sizes follow seats: S 4, M 6, L 8.
2. Run `python3 tools/render_blockout.py`. `layout.json` gets the room, its pad and seats; `anchors.json` gets the pad and tile reference. The self-checks must pass.
3. If the archetype x size already has a tile, you are done. Preview a growth plan with `python3 tools/render_blockout.py --rooms N` (writes nothing).

## Add an archetype

1. Add it under `params.archetypes` (wall height, default size). Re-run the tool; `tiles/<archetype>-<S|M|L>.png` and `-front.png` appear as blockouts.
2. Paint the tile image-to-image from the blockout, following `style.md` prompt rules: blockout attached with `-i`, prompt first, the preservation suffix from `keyframes/PROMPTS.md`, "no warm colours", "no text, no labels, no numbers, no logos", transparent background. Keep the canvas, anchor and the front layer's footprint unchanged.
3. Cut the painted front layer with the blockout's `-front.png` as its mask, then re-run the tool and the checks.

A room larger than the tile frame (`params.tile.max_d`, `max_h`) needs a deliberate frame change and a re-cut of every tile.
