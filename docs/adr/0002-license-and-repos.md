# ADR 0002: Apache-2.0 public core, private engineering depth

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-10-22

## Decision
The platform core is public under Apache-2.0. Discipline packs, tuned deadbands, check criteria, Ascertain integration and client templates live in a private FlyFission repository. Outside contributions require a DCO sign-off now and a contributor license agreement before they open.

## Alternatives considered
MIT (no patent grant), AGPL or source-available licenses (deter the enterprise and open-source audience).

## Consequences
The free and paid line follows one rule: what an individual engineer needs is public; what a QA manager, CIO or regulator needs may be paid. Free features never move behind the paywall.
