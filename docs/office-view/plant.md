# The Plant

The Plant is the default look of the office view. It draws one facility in plan: the same 13 places as the office floor (`FigurePlace` in `spec/api.tsp` is unchanged), arranged in zones with plain names. The design and the reviews that shaped it are in [the plant view design](../superpowers/specs/2026-11-23-plant-view-design.md).

| Zone | Places (label on the plate) |
|---|---|
| Plant fence | Your office (Main control room), Verification (Independent verification), Interface (Work control), Review (Review board) |
| Reactor building | No seats. The core and its control rods. |
| Auxiliary building | The six cube sets, each a Work bay |
| Site, outside the fence | Bench (Staging yard), Document control (Records vault), Quarantine (Hold yard) |

## Instruments

Each instrument is a pure function in `apps/web/src/features/office/plant-instruments.ts`, and each says when it does not know: a read that has not arrived or failed is never drawn as zero, dark or "no SCRAM".

- **Annunciator**: four windows, Waiting on you (the control plane's `needs_me`), Stuck, Input changed and SCRAM. Dark while the plant is normal. A drill is named but never lights the SCRAM window.
- **Core**: lit while any shown agent is working. A count in words, never a power level. In presenting mode it says how many figures are hidden.
- **Control rods**: withdrawn with no SCRAM in force; all inserted for an organization SCRAM (the stop time is claimed only once recorded); one bank for project SCRAMs ("Other work continues"); dashed for a drill; not drawn when SCRAM status is unavailable.

## Layout and art

`plant-layout.ts` holds the geometry and `validatePlantLayout` checks it: zones nest, every region sits in its zone, nothing sits in the reactor building, every door meets a walkway, every queue lies on a walkway of its own zone, and the gate graph is connected. The art is generated by `scripts/build-plant-theme.ts` (run through `scripts/build-office-themes.ts`); never edit the files under `public/office-themes/plant/` or `themes/plant.ts` by hand. The core is application code, not theme art, so a theme cannot fake it.

## Narrow screens and the list

Below the width where the drawing fits beside the card, the view opens on the list: every figure grouped by zone and place. The Plant and List switch is always available.

## Archived office themes

The fifteen office themes and the bright harness theme stay available under **Advanced**, **Archived office themes**. The old `/office` address redirects to `/plant`.
