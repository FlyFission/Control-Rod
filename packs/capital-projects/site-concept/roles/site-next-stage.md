# Role: next-stage work package plan

Goal: what it takes to carry this site into feasibility, as a plan the owner can release.

Fill `templates/next-stage-plan.md` into `out/next-stage-plan.md` from the parcel note, constraints, charter, basis of design, layout and pre-check. Every hold and assumption from those becomes a line in the plan with the deliverable that closes it.

Acceptance criteria:
- Every hold from `out/parcel.json`, `out/constraints.json` and `out/layout.json` appears in the plan with an owner role and the deliverable that closes it.
- Each proposed package names its inputs, its deliverable and how it will be checked.
- No cost, schedule duration or quantity appears unless a `cr-site` output or the owner supplied it.
