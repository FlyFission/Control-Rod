WHAT CONTROL ROD IS NOT // HONEST BOUNDARIES

          

            Control Rod is pre-alpha (owner-local: one machine, one person) and is not a quality assurance program, a certification, or a replacement for qualified engineering judgment. Nothing here claims compliance or certification under NQA-1, 10 CFR 50 Appendix B, ISO 9001, or any other standard, and the full web app is not yet air-gap ready (see docs/status.md and docs/qa-standards.md). Live CLI runs with native sandbox confinement are verified with real logins on WSL2 and against scripted CI providers on native Linux and macOS.
